# Takeaway

Core claim: CCS works because of the displacement information in its contrast-pair data, not because of its specific loss formula — a simple Midpoint-Displacement loss reproduces CCS and, retuned, beats it.

Supporting detail: The exact loss formulation is largely interchangeable among high-accuracy probes; the trade-off between displacement variance and midpoint variance is what defines CCS's target.
