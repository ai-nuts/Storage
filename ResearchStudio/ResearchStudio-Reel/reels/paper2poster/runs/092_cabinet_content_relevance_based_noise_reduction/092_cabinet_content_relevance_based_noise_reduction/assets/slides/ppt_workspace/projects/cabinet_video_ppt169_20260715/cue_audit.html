05method0.10-16.18saccepted0.99pptx:s05_sh61CABINET works in a sequence of steps. The table is flattened into a linear string with header and row markers and embedded together with the question. 05method16.60-42.20saccepted0.99pptx:s05_sh62Because there are no ground-truth annotations for which cells are relevant, relevance is treated as a latent variable estimated through variational inference, and the encoder's representation space is shaped by a clustering loss that groups tokens into relevant and non-relevant, a separation loss that pushes the two cluster centroids apart, and a sparsification loss that drives irrelevant scores toward zero and relevant scores toward one.
05method42.61-54.74saccepted0.99pptx:s05_sh63Each table token's embedding is multiplied by its relevance score, so noisy cells are softly suppressed rather than deleted, and the whole system trains end-to-end through the answer-generation cross-entropy loss.
