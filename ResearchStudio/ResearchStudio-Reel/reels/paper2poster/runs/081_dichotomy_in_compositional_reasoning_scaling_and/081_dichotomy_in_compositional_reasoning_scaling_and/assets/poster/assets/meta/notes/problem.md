# Problem

Core claim: Can an LLM that learns two simple tasks in-context automatically solve their composition, an unseen composite task, without ever seeing composite examples? This basic generalization is trivial for humans but largely untested for LLMs.

Supporting detail: Compositional ability, solving unseen complex tasks that combine simpler ones, is viewed as essential for general intelligence, yet how LLMs handle composite tasks not seen in pretraining remains open and underexplored.

Narration: Suppose a model learned two simple tasks in-context, capitalizing certain words and swapping others. Given an input needing both, can it combine the skills? For humans this is trivial, yet even GPT-4 and Claude 3 often fail.
