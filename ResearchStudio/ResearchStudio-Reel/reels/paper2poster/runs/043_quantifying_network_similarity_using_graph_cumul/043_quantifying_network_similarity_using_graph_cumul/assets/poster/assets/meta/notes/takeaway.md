# Takeaway

Core claim: When comparing networks with subgraph or motif densities, convert them to graph cumulants: you gain substantially more statistical power, an accurate analytic χ² null, and the ability to test even a single observed graph, all at no extra computational cost.

Narration: The bottom line is a practical one. Whenever you find yourself analyzing networks through subgraph or motif densities, take one extra step and convert them into graph cumulants. You lose nothing in computational cost, but you gain sharply higher statistical power, a trustworthy chi-squared null distribution for controlling error rates, and the ability to draw conclusions even from a single observed network. In short, graph cumulants offer a principled, more natural first layer of features for comparing the local structure of networks.
