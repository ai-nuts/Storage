# Headline Numbers

Core claim: 638,180 human face-similarity judgments over 4,921 faces; 22 interpretable dimensions retained at 61.9% validation accuracy; annotator-swap accuracy drop 61.7% to 52.8%; continuous attributes recovered without any semantic training labels.

Supporting detail: 128-dimensional ResNet18 embeddings; 24,060 same-stimuli and 80,300 novel-stimuli evaluation judgments.

Narration: The headline numbers tell the whole story. Six hundred thirty-eight thousand human judgments gathered over four thousand nine hundred twenty-one distinct faces. Twenty-two interpretable embedding dimensions retained at nearly sixty-two percent validation accuracy. And when annotator identities are scrambled, accuracy falls from sixty-one point seven down to fifty-two point eight percent, all achieved without ever training on a single semantic label.
