# Dataset / Benchmark

Core claim: AVFS contains 638,180 quality-controlled odd-one-out triplet judgments over 4,921 near-frontal face images, each judgment tagged with the annotator's identifier and self-identified demographic attributes.

Supporting detail: Evaluation uses held-out same-stimuli triplets (24,060 judgments), novel-stimuli triplets (80,300 judgments), and comparisons against semantically trained baselines on CelebA and FairFace attributes.

Narration: The AVFS dataset comprises six hundred thirty-eight thousand quality-controlled triplet judgments over four thousand nine hundred twenty-one near-frontal face images. Every single judgment carries the annotator's identifier and self-identified demographic attributes. The authors evaluate on held-out judgments over the same stimuli, on eighty thousand judgments over entirely novel faces, and against attribute-recognition baselines trained on CelebA and FairFace semantic labels.
