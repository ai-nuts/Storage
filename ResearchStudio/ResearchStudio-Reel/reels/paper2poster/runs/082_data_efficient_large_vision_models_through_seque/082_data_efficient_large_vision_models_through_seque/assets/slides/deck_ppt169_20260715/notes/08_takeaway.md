The takeaway is refreshingly simple. You do not need a three-billion-parameter model and hundreds of billions of tokens to build a capable autoregressive vision model. Two classical techniques, data augmentation to rebalance long-tailed tasks and knowledge distillation to compress a large teacher, together let compact models trained on limited data perform strongly across segmentation, pose estimation, and deraining. That an eighty-million-parameter version even reaches eighty-three percent accuracy on ImageNet points toward efficient, deployable generalist vision models that unify generation and understanding.
