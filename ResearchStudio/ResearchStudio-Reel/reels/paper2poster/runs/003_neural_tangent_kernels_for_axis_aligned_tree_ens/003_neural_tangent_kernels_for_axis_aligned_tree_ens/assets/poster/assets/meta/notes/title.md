# Title

Soft tree ensembles are gradient-trained decision trees that rival neural networks on tabular data, yet their training has lacked theory. This ICLR 2022 paper introduces the Tree Neural Tangent Kernel, or TNTK, describing an ensemble of infinitely many soft trees. With it, the authors prove global training convergence, show oblivious trees match ordinary ones, and reveal that very deep trees degenerate into a flat kernel, all validated on ninety datasets.
