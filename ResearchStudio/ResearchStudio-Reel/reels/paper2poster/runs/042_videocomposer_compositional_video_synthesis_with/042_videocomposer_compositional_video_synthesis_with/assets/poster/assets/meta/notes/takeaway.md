# Takeaway

Core claim: By treating a video as a composition of textual, spatial, and temporal conditions, and by pairing compressed-video motion vectors with a unified STC-encoder, VideoComposer delivers flexible, controllable video synthesis with strong inter-frame consistency and no loss in text-to-video quality.

Supporting detail: The same model can be driven by text, sketches, depth, masks, reference video, or even two hand-drawn strokes, making motion an explicit, user-editable dimension of video generation.

Narration: The takeaway: video generation becomes more controllable when you treat a video as a composition of conditions and give motion an explicit signal. VideoComposer combines compressed-video motion vectors with a Spatio-Temporal Condition encoder, so users steer content and motion together, from text, sketches, and depth to masks, reference videos, or two strokes, while keeping frames consistent.
