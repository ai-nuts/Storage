# Key Result

Core claim: On MSR-VTT, VideoComposer reaches an FVD of 580 and a CLIPSIM of 0.2932 in the zero-shot setting, competitive with state-of-the-art text-to-video methods and better than its own first-stage text-to-video pre-training (FVD 803), showing compositional training does not sacrifice text-to-video quality.

Supporting detail: Qualitatively, VideoComposer produces videos that faithfully follow supplied sketches, depth sequences, motion vectors, masks, and reference styles, and can animate two simple strokes into coherent motion and shape.

Narration: On MSR-VTT, VideoComposer achieves a Fréchet Video Distance of five hundred eighty and a CLIP similarity of zero point two nine three two, zero-shot. That is competitive with leading text-to-video systems, and it improves over the model's first-stage pre-training, which scored eight hundred three. So adding compositional, multi-condition control costs nothing in quality, while videos follow the sketches, depth, motion, and styles users provide.
