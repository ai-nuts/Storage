# Key Result

Core claim: On node classification CoCN attains the best average rank of 1.83 over six datasets, topping Chameleon (79.17%), Squirrel (72.95%) and Cornell (86.22%); on graph classification it wins five of six datasets, including COLLAB (86.15%), IMDB-MULTI (56.32%) and NCI1 (82.89%).

Supporting detail: On the filtered Chameleon and Squirrel datasets CoCN ranks first among 18 models (79.61% / 73.27%), showing it is far less sensitive to node-duplicate leakage than the baselines.

Narration: Results are strong across the board. On node classification, CoCN gets the best average rank, one point eight three, topping Chameleon at seventy-nine percent, Squirrel at seventy-three, and Cornell at eighty-six. On graph classification, it wins five of six datasets, including COLLAB and IMDB-Multi. And on filtered Chameleon and Squirrel, with duplicates removed, it ranks first of eighteen models, so its edge is not data leakage.
