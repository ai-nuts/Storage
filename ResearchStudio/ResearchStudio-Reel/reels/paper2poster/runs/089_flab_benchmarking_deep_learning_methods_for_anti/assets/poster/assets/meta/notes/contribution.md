# Contribution

Core claim: The authors present FLAb, the largest therapeutic antibody design benchmark to date, curating 17 mutational landscapes with 13,384 fitness measurements across six developability properties, and use it to benchmark six pretrained protein models against physics-based Rosetta.

Supporting detail: All FLAb data are released openly for community contribution, and the study dissects how wild-type origin, architecture, training-data composition, parameter size, and evolutionary signal each affect performance.

Narration: To fill this gap, the authors build FLAb, the Fitness Landscape for Antibodies, the largest therapeutic antibody design benchmark assembled so far. It curates seventeen mutational landscapes with more than thirteen thousand experimental fitness measurements, spanning six developability properties. Using this benchmark, they evaluate six widely adopted pretrained protein models and compare them to physics-based Rosetta. Just as importantly, all of the FLAb data are released openly so the community can keep expanding it.
