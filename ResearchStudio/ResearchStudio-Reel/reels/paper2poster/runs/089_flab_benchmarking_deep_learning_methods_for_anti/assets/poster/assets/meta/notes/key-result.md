# Key Result

Core claim: No model correlates well with all six properties, and performance varies across datasets of the same property. ProGen2-Small achieved the most top performances (best on seven datasets), with ProGen2-Medium, ProGen2-OAS, ESM-IF, and Rosetta Energy tied for second (top on six datasets each).

Supporting detail: Sequence-based models on average outperform structure-based models across all six landscapes, with thermostability showing the largest gap.

Narration: The headline result is a reality check. No model correlates well with all six properties, and even for a single property, performance swings widely from one dataset to another. ProGen2-Small was the most frequent winner, coming out on top for seven datasets, while ProGen2-Medium, ProGen2-OAS, ESM-IF, and Rosetta energy tied just behind at six each. Notably, sequence-based models on average beat structure-based ones across every landscape, with the biggest gap on thermostability, suggesting that raw sequence signal still carries most of the predictive power here.
