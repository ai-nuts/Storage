# Headline Numbers

Core claim: - QM9 R² MAE: 0.342 (G2N2) vs 3.78 (PPGN), single-target — a ~11× reduction. - QM9 mean epoch time: 98 s (G2N2) vs 129 s (PPGN), single-target.

Supporting detail: - Spectral band-pass R²: 0.8206 (G2N2) vs 0.1041 (PPGN). - TUD: better than rank 2 on 5 of 6 datasets (MUTAG 92.5±5.5, Proteins 80.1±3.7).

Narration: A few numbers capture the impact. On QM9's R-squared target, error falls from three-point-seven-eight to zero-point-three-four-two, roughly an eleven-fold reduction, and the model does it in ninety-eight seconds per epoch versus PPGN's one hundred twenty-nine. On the spectral band-pass task, its R-squared score of zero-point-eight-two towers over PPGN's zero-point-one-zero. And on the TUD classification suite it lands better than second place on five of six datasets, including ninety-two-and-a-half percent on MUTAG.
