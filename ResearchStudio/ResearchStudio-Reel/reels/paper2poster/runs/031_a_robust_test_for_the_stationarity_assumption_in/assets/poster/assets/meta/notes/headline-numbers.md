# Headline Numbers

Core claim: - Correct change-point detection sustained up to state dimension dS = 30, versus dS = 1 for CUSUM-RL. - Nominal significance α = 0.05 maintained across all four simulations and both real-data specialties. - Real IHS data: change point detected for Internal Medicine interns at week 16, none for Family Practice.

Supporting detail: ε = 0.1 boundary removal; 5000 bootstrap samples (B=1000 for real data); results aggregated over 100–500 replications.

Narration: A few numbers anchor the impact. The test sustains correct change-point detection all the way up to a state dimension of thirty, whereas the closest competitor works only at dimension one. It maintains the nominal five-percent significance level across all four simulations and both real-data specialties. And on the real Intern Health Study data, it flags a genuine change point for Internal Medicine interns at week sixteen, while correctly finding no change for the Family Practice group. These results come from thousands of bootstrap samples and hundreds of replications per setting.
