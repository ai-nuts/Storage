# Motivation

Core claim: Humans solve families of tasks, like dicing any vegetable, because their goal representation is invariant to irrelevant factors and equivariant to the factors that differ between analogous tasks.

Supporting detail: Prior bisimulation and representation-learning methods focus on robustness to distractors for a single task, not on transferring skills across analogous goals.

Narration: Consider dicing a carrot versus a radish. The objects differ, but the skill and the change from start to goal are the same. Good representations are invariant to irrelevant details and equivariant to what varies. Prior bisimulation handled distractors for one task; we lift it to families of tasks.
