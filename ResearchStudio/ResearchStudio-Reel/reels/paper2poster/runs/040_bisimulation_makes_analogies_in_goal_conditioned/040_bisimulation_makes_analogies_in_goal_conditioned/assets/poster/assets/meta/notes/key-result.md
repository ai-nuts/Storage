# Key Result

Core claim: GCB achieves the best final success rate on 5 of 6 task settings, and is the strongest method on every distractor-augmented task, e.g. 0.448 on Drawer+VD, 0.322 on BD+VD, and 0.403 / 0.303 on Analogy / Analogy+VD.

Supporting detail: On the analogy tasks GCB is the only representation that supports goal inference from an analogous pair, roughly doubling the next-best baseline (CPV) on Analogy (0.403 vs 0.176).

Narration: Across six settings, GCB is top on five, and wins on every distractor task. It reaches about forty-five percent on Drawer with distractors and thirty-two on Button-and-Drawer. On analogy tasks, where the goal is inferred from an example, it roughly doubles the next-best baseline.
