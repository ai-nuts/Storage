# Headline Numbers

Core claim: - CIFAR-10 Top-1: 60.4% → 84.3% with homeostatic loss (asymmetric hEP) - Only 4.3-point gap to the symmetric architecture on CIFAR-10 - ImageNet 32×32: 31.4% Top-1 / 55.2% Top-5 (first hEP result at this scale) - Fashion MNIST at |β|=0.5: classic EP 38.4% error vs. 14.3% with the N=6 Cauchy estimate

Supporting detail: All CIFAR results average three seeds; the ImageNet 32×32 result is the first reported for equilibrium propagation on this benchmark.

Narration: To put the numbers in one place: on CIFAR-10, the homeostatic loss lifts asymmetric EP from sixty point four to eighty-four point three percent accuracy, closing the gap to the symmetric network to just four point three points. On ImageNet thirty-two by thirty-two, EP reaches thirty-one point four percent Top-1 and fifty-five point two percent Top-5, the first result of its kind. And on Fashion MNIST, using a large nudge, the exact holomorphic estimate cuts error from thirty-eight point four percent down to fourteen point three percent.
