# Dataset / Benchmark

Core claim: This is a purely theoretical paper: there are no datasets, benchmarks, or empirical experiments. All results are mathematical theorems proven under stated assumptions.

Supporting detail: The loss functions considered are general smooth (continuously differentiable), non-negative, possibly non-convex functions whose gradients are Lipschitz; example families satisfying the assumptions include periodic and polynomial functions such as sin²(x) and (x−1)(x−2)(x−3)(x−4).

Narration: This work is entirely theoretical: no datasets, benchmarks, or experiments, only proven theorems. The losses are non-negative and differentiable with Lipschitz gradients, but possibly non-convex, like sine-squared and quartic polynomials.
