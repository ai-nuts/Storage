# Key Result

Core claim: Under mild assumptions, mSGD iterates converge almost surely to a connected set of stationary points (Theorem 1), and standard norm-form AdaGrad iterates do the same (Theorem 3), both for non-convex losses.

Supporting detail: These almost-sure asymptotic results are strictly stronger than the subsequence-convergence and time-average results they extend from prior literature.

Narration: The headline is two almost-sure convergence theorems. Theorem 1: momentum SGD iterates converge to a connected set of stationary points. Theorem 3: standard AdaGrad does the same. Both strengthen earlier subsequence and time-average results.
