# Contribution

Core claim: The paper proves almost-sure asymptotic convergence of mSGD to a connected set of stationary points, quantifies its convergence rate to explain the acceleration over SGD, and proves almost-sure asymptotic convergence of standard norm-form AdaGrad.

Supporting detail: Results hold for possibly non-convex loss functions under relaxed noise assumptions, extending subsequence and time-average convergence results in the literature to the stronger asymptotic sense.

Narration: Three contributions. First, momentum SGD iterates converge almost surely to a connected set of stationary points, even for non-convex losses. Second, it quantifies mSGD's rate, explaining momentum's acceleration. Third, standard norm-form AdaGrad also converges almost surely.
