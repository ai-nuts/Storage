# Title

This paper, presented at ICML 2024, tackles one-shot imitation learning when the world changes after the demonstration is given. The authors introduce Deep Demonstration Tracing, or DDT, a method that lets an imitator agent adaptively trace the right states in a single demonstration while recovering from unforeseen obstacles. DDT combines a purpose-built demonstration transformer with a meta-reinforcement-learning training scheme, and it outperforms prior one-shot imitation methods across a new maze navigation benchmark and several robotics tasks.
