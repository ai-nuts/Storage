# Headline Numbers

Core claim: Evaluated across 3 distribution-shift types (size, concept, covariate) and 3 safety tasks (calibration, generalization-gap prediction, OOD detection), against 4 UQ baselines (Vanilla, DEns, Temp, MCD).

Supporting detail: 3 anchoring variants (node-feature, intermediate-MPNN, READOUT) plus a pretrained partial-stochasticity variant; single-model method (no ensemble needed).

Narration: In numbers, G-Delta-UQ is a single-model method offering three anchoring variants plus a pretrained partial-stochasticity option. It is evaluated across three distinct types of distribution shift and three safety-critical downstream tasks, and compared against four established uncertainty baselines including deep ensembles. Despite requiring only a single model, it consistently matches or outperforms these baselines on calibration, out-of-distribution detection, and generalization gap prediction.
