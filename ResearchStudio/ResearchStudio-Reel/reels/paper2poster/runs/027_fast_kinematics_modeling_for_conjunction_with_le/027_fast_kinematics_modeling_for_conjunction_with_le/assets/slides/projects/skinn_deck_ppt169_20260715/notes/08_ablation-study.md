As a proof-of-concept workshop paper, this work does not present a formal ablation study. Instead, performance is characterized by the distribution of per-pixel errors across the five hundred test images, and by comparing inference on GPU versus CPU, where the GPU is about ten times faster. The analysis also distinguishes the innermost two-arcsecond region, where errors stay within one percent, from the outer parts of the image, where errors can rise to a few percent but the data provides little constraint anyway.
