# Contribution

Core claim: The paper contributes (1) spatial Graph Attention (sGAT), a GNN that self-attends over node and edge attributes and encodes 3D spatial structure; (2) Graph Attention Policy Network (GAPN), an attentional policy for a dynamic fragment-based action space trained with PPO; and (3) a random-network-distillation curiosity bonus, together forming DGAPN.

Supporting detail: DGAPN reduces the complexity of paths to chemical synthesis while achieving state-of-the-art binding affinity on a SARS-CoV-2 target.

Narration: DGAPN makes three contributions. First, spatial Graph Attention, a GNN that self-attends over node and edge attributes and encodes 3D structure via an inverse distance matrix. Second, the Graph Attention Policy Network, an attentional policy over a dynamic fragment action space trained with PPO. Third, a random-network-distillation curiosity bonus giving innovation rewards for high-affinity, synthesizable molecules.
