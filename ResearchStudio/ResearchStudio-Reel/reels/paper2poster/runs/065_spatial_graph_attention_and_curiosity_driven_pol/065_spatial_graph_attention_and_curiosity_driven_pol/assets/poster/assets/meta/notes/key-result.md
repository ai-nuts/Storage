# Key Result

Core claim: On single-objective docking against NSP15, DGAPN reaches a −10.07 top and −6.77 mean docking score, significantly beating all five baselines (REINVENT, JTVAE, GCPN, MolDQN, MARS), with a p-value of 8.55 × 10⁻²⁰⁹ over the second-best model (MolDQN) under Welch's t-test.

Supporting detail: In constrained optimization DGAPN gives the largest property improvements across all similarity thresholds versus MolDQN and MARS; in multi-objective mode (ω = 0.6) it attains QED 0.72 and SA 2.20 while keeping a −5.69 mean docking score better than every single-objective baseline.

Narration: On docking against NSP15, DGAPN clearly beats five state-of-the-art baselines, reaching a best score near minus ten and a mean of minus six point seven seven, well ahead of MolDQN, with an astronomically small p-value under Welch's t-test. It also wins constrained optimization at every similarity threshold, and in multi-objective mode trades slight affinity for markedly better drug-likeness and synthesizability.
