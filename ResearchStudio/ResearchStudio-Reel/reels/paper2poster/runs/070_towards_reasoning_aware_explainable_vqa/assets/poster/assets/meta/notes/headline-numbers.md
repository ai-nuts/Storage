# Headline Numbers

Core claim: - 77.49% VQA score on GQA-REX (maintains SOTA while explaining) - 71.48% VQA score on VQA-E - 65.16% of generated explanations judged by humans to lead to the correct answer - ~60.5% of cases give both a correct answer and a valid explanation

Supporting detail: Human study covered 4735 unique VQA-E validation image-question pairs, 3 annotators each (14,205 responses from 111 subjects); 93.12% of ground-truth explanations were judged valid.

Narration: Four numbers summarize the paper's impact. The model achieves a seventy-seven point four nine percent VQA score on GQA-REX and seventy-one point four eight percent on VQA-E, both at state-of-the-art level. In the human study, sixty-five point one six percent of the generated explanations were judged by annotators to genuinely lead to the predicted answer. And in roughly sixty point five percent of cases, the model both predicts the correct answer and generates a valid explanation for it. The human study itself was substantial: four thousand seven hundred thirty-five unique image-question pairs from the VQA-E validation set, each rated by three annotators, for over fourteen thousand responses from one hundred eleven subjects.
