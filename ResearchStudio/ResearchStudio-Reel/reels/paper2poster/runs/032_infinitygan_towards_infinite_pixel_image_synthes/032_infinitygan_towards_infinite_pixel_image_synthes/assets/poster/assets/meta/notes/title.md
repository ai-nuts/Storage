# Title

InfinityGAN asks a bold question: can a model synthesize images of arbitrary, even infinite size, while training only on small patches on a single consumer GPU? Existing high-resolution GANs fail because compute, memory, and data all scale with output resolution. InfinityGAN breaks that coupling, disentangling an image into global appearance, local structure, and fine texture, and generating any region patch-by-patch from continuous coordinates, seamless, globally consistent, and unbounded in size.
