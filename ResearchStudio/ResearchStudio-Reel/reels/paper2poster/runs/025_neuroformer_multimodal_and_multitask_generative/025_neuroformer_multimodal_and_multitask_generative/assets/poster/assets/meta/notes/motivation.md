# Motivation

Core claim: Large pretrained transformers transformed vision and language by learning general representations from raw data; the authors argue the same generative, self-supervised recipe can unlock scalable analysis of neural spiking data.

Supporting detail: Prior spike models are often task-specific, single-modality, or scale poorly with the number of neurons and features, limiting transfer to new downstream tasks.

Narration: Large pretrained models in vision and language point a way forward: they learn general representations self-supervised from raw data, then transfer with little labeling. The authors ask whether reframing spike analysis as generative prediction can likewise scale, fuse modalities, and transfer to behavior decoding.
