# Takeaway

Core claim: Treating neural spiking data as an autoregressive, multimodal generation problem lets a single self-supervised transformer recover circuit connectivity and transfer to behavior decoding with few-shot fine-tuning, bringing the pretraining paradigm to systems neuroscience.

Supporting detail: Neuroformer paves the way for scaling neuroscience foundation models and potentially connecting them to large language models.

Narration: The takeaway: the pretraining paradigm that reshaped vision and language can also work for the brain. Treating spikes as autoregressive multimodal generation, one self-supervised transformer recovers a circuit's directed connectivity and decodes behavior from few examples, pointing toward scalable neuroscience foundation models.
