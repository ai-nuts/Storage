# Title

Tabular language models could unlock the spreadsheets buried inside industrial companies, but they need labeled data, and only scarce experts can annotate these technical tables. Presented at NeurIPS 2022 by Ringsquandl and Koleva from Siemens, this paper asks: can active learning cut that labeling cost for sub-cell entity recognition? Their headline finding is that a batch-diverse gradient method, BADGE, matches full-training performance with far fewer labels, while forcing maximum table diversity actually hurts.
