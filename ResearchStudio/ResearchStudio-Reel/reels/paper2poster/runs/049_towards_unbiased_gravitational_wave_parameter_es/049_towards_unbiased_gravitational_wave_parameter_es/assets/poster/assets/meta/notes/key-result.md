# Key Result

Core claim: SLIC recovers the five varied source parameters of the injected signal with high credibility, and its posterior has smaller variance than the standard Gaussian-likelihood posterior; the model also reproduces the real LIGO PSD, including narrow spectral lines.

Supporting detail: From 1024 sampled noise realizations, SLIC's power spectral density closely matches the Welch estimate from real LIGO data and resolves spectral lines more finely thanks to exposure to more data (11 h vs 1 h).

Narration: The results are encouraging. Drawing one thousand twenty-four noise realizations from SLIC, its power spectrum matches a Welch estimate from real LIGO data, and it resolves narrow spectral lines even more finely thanks to more training data. Injecting a known signal, SLIC recovers all five varied parameters with high credibility, and its posterior is tighter than the Gaussian-likelihood one.
