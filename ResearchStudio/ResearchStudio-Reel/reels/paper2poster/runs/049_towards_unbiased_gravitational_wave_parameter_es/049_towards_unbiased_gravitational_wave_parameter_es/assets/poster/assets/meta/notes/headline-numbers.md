# Headline Numbers

Core claim: - 11 hours of real LIGO-Hanford noise used to train the score-based model - 1024 synthetic noise realizations sampled to validate the learned PSD - 5 source parameters (M, η, dL, tc, ϕc) recovered from the injected signal

Supporting detail: - Data sampled at 4096 Hz, split into 4 s segments; likelihood integration from 20 Hz - Injected signal: chirp mass M = 29 M⊙, η = 0.2495, dL = 400 Mpc, χ₁ = χ₂ = 0

Narration: A few numbers anchor the demonstration. Eleven hours of real LIGO-Hanford noise train the score model. One thousand twenty-four synthetic realizations validate the learned power spectrum. Inference recovers five source parameters: chirp mass, mass ratio, luminosity distance, coalescence time, and phase. Data are sampled at four thousand ninety-six hertz in four-second segments.
