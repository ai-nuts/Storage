# Dataset / Benchmark

Core claim: Evaluated on four standard datasets: FFHQ (70k human faces) and the three AFHQ splits — Cats (5653), Dogs (5239), and Wild (5238) — reporting FID at 256² resolution against 11 state-of-the-art 3D-GAN baselines.

Supporting detail: FFHQ provides challenging backgrounds and poses; AFHQ tests generalization beyond faces to animal categories with more varied shape and topology.

Narration: StyleMorph is evaluated on four widely used image datasets. FFHQ contributes seventy thousand centered human face photos with a range of challenging backgrounds and poses. The AFHQ collection adds animal faces in three splits: roughly fifty-six hundred cats, fifty-two hundred dogs, and fifty-two hundred wild animals. The authors report Frechet Inception Distance at two-fifty-six resolution, comparing against eleven state-of-the-art 3D-aware GAN baselines, so the results test both photorealism on faces and the ability to generalize to animal categories that classical morphable models never covered.
